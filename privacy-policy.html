GeoIPTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What the app stores

GeoIPTV stores two settings on your TV only: the playlist link you enter and your interface language. They are not sent to us. We do not ask for your name, email, account or payment details.

Playlists and channels

GeoIPTV does not provide any channels or content. The app loads the playlist link you enter and plays streams directly from the servers listed in it. Those servers see your TV's IP address, as with any internet video.

Advertising

The app shows video ads served by a third-party ad network. To deliver and count ads, the network may receive your IP address, device and app information, and whether an ad was played. This data is handled under the ad network's own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your TV's settings (Settings → General → Additional Settings → Advertisement, depending on the model).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
